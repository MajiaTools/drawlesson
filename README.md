## What it does

- **Original step-by-step lessons** from the AR Drawing collection — start with a cat or a rose, then explore more subjects
- **Red lines show what to draw next** — start with the outline, connect the main shapes, then add the details
- **Turn your picture into a drawing lesson**: upload a reference (PNG / JPG / WEBP, up to 12 MB), choose the number of steps, and generate an AI step sheet with written guidance
- Small steps, visible progress — practise one step at a time and review the result before you draw

## How to use

1. Open https://drawlesson.com and pick a lesson — the cat or rose is a good start
2. Follow each picture in order; red lines show what to draw next
3. Or upload your own image and generate an AI step sheet to practise with
